# Lede

Founded in 1855 as Sebastopol and renamed for George C. Yount in 1867, this postage-stamp town—some 1.5 square miles—now reads like a living anthology of modern Napa cuisine. Thomas Keller’s French Laundry still anchors an empire that includes Bouchon Bistro, Bouchon Bakery, Ad Hoc and Addendum; Bottega, Lucy and a wave of chef-driven arrivals keep Washington Street buzzing between meal times.

Downtown alone counts more than a dozen tasting rooms, and Oak Knoll plus Stags Leap sit minutes away, bringing fog-cooled elegance to Cabernet, Chardonnay and ancient-vine heritage varieties. Park once, walk everywhere, accept that the bakery line is part of the performance art.

# Where to Taste

## Featured Wineries

### Stewart Cellars
- **Address:** 6752 Washington Street
- **Website:** stewartcellars.com

Ken Fulk and Arcanum Architecture conjured a residential-feeling salon—vintage furniture, courtyard firepits, Nomad Library tastings for collectors chasing Beckstoffer vineyard designates. Drop-ins stay easy; deep dives reward planning.

### Clos du Val
- **Address:** 5330 Silverado Trail
- **Website:** closduval.com

The Goelet family’s Hirondelle House reframes a Stags Leap classic: tall windows, patio tastings above the hedge rows and flights that connect Judgment of Paris history to today’s polished Cabernet program.

### Lewis Cellars
- **Address:** 6320 Silverado Trail
- **Website:** lewiscellars.com

The Wonderful Company’s blockbuster relocation from Oak Knoll pairs opulent interiors with plush outdoor terraces—a Napa valley chapter dressed like a London drawing room with Mayacamas views.

### Hendry Winery
- **Address:** 3104 Redwood Road
- **Website:** hendrywines.com

Hike-with-Hendry mornings and porch pours still define this ranch since 1939—no marble foyer, just family storytelling, Zinfandel heritage blocks and Cabernet that overdelivers for the understated setting.

# Where to Eat

## Ad Hoc + Addendum
- **Address:** 6476 Washington Street
- **Website:** thomaskeller.com/adhoc

The daily-changing family-style menu—fried chicken, braises, caviar bumps—remains the attainable Keller experience. Addendum supplies picnic ribs and fried chicken when you need something handheld on the lawn.

## Clementine
- **Address:** 6525 Washington Street
- **Website:** clementineyountville.com

Mediterranean energy, olive-shaded patio Aperol spritzes and sommelier-led wine flights favor Napa Cabernets without snobbery—ideal when you want inventive plates without a jacket requirement.

## The French Laundry
- **Address:** 6640 Washington Street
- **Website:** thomaskeller.com/tfl

Grand Award service, the culinary garden across the street and a reservation chase known worldwide—book the moment dates drop and surrender to the choreography.

## Breakfast, Coffee & Snacks

### Bouchon Bakery
- **Address:** 6528 Washington Street
- **Website:** thomaskeller.com/bouchon-bakery

Pastries, coffee and takeaway picnics that make the queue part of the ritual—arrive early if you need macarons before a balloon ride.

# Where to Stay

## Featured Hotels

### Bardessono
- **Address:** 6526 Yount Street
- **Website:** bardessono.com

LEED Platinum suites with private patios, spa-level baths and Lucy Restaurant downstairs exemplify eco-luxury—walk to Kellerland from a property that feels like a minimalist retreat.

### The Estate Yountville
- **Address:** 6481 Washington Street
- **Website:** theestateyountville.com

Twenty-two acres stitch Hotel Villagio, Vintage House, a villa and spa into a village-within-a-village—the default choice when your group wants pools, dining variety and Washington Street at your doorstep.

### Sttupa Estate
- **Address:** 638 Silverado Trail
- **Website:** sttupaestate.com

Formerly Poetry Inn, now a Sullivan-backed aerie with poet-named suites, fireplaces and Stags Leap views—ideal for honeymoons or anyone who prefers hillside silence to sidewalk bustle.

# Sidebar: Public Art & Slow Strolls

*Why Yountville rewards travelers who ditch the car.*

**Walk the tiles**

By Aaron Romano

Sculpture gardens, Veterans Memorial murals and vineyard-adjacent paseos turn the short walk from Stewart to Chandon into an open-air museum. Pair a morning bakery run with an afternoon Stags Leap appointment—then loop back for Regiis Ova caviar or Ciccio’s spritz without reparking. Small footprint, outsized appetite.
