# Lede

In the late 1960s, Pritchard Hill was isolated and essentially forgotten. Today it remains quiet, but no longer anonymous: a tight cluster of Cabernet houses works iron-red and yellow-tinged volcanic soils as high as about 1,800 feet. Driving the long switchbacks east of Rutherford, you pass vineyards, a few architectural statements and homes that explain why collectors speak of this mountainside in hushed tones. The wines—typically structured, mineral and slow to unfold—reward patience in the cellar as much as curiosity on the road.

Architecture fans should note the Howard Backen Estate on Long Ranch Road: originally the Nelson-Johnson residence near Ovid, reimagined as an ultra-private rental with concierge access to neighboring estates and Thomas Keller Restaurant Group dining. It is not a substitute for a winery passport, but it is a dramatic home base if the hill is your reason for visiting Napa.

# Where to Taste

## Featured Wineries

### David Arthur Vineyards
- **Address:** 210 Long Ranch Road
- **Website:** davidarthur.com

Don Long assembled roughly 1,000 acres here in the 1950s; today David Long and the next generations continue the family thread. Winemaker Nile Zacherle crafts muscular, mineral-laced Cabernet that many cite as textbook Pritchard Hill: deep fruit, savory tension and a finish that unfurls over minutes, not seconds.

### Chappellet Winery
- **Address:** 1581 Sage Canyon Road
- **Website:** chappellet.com

Donn and Molly Chappellet founded the estate in 1967; legends from Philip Togni to Cathy Corison passed through before Phillip Corallo-Titus anchored the cellar. Today Cyril Chappellet and family still farm this perch above the fog, balancing history with renovated hospitality and some of the most photographed views on the crest.

### Continuum Estate
- **Address:** 1677 Sage Canyon Road
- **Website:** continuumestate.com

Tim Mondavi’s post-Opus project, now a family affair with the next generation at work in the vineyards and cellar, explores how Bordeaux varieties behave on Pritchard’s fractured rock. The new barrel and hospitality spaces signal more chef collaborations, sunrise hikes and library access—experiences tuned to people who already know why elevation matters.

# Where to Eat

## Long-arrival picnics & Rutherford resets
- **Address:** Oakville Grocery, 7856 St. Helena Highway
- **Website:** oakvillegrocery.com

There is no village square on the hill, so most visitors sandwich tastings with meals down on the valley floor. Grab sandwiches and salads at Oakville Grocery or plan a late lunch at Brix or Mustards after the winding descent—your designated driver earns the extra bread course.

# Sidebar: The Pritchard Hill Pilgrimage

*Editor’s notebook: how to stitch three mountain tastings into one judicious day.*

**Pacing the climb**

By James Molesworth

Start early at Chappellet when the light stripes the Vaca range, then continue to Continuum while the fog still pools below. David Arthur’s intimate tastings slot well before or after, depending on appointment rhythm. Leave buffer time for narrow roads, photo stops, and the reality that each host pours wines meant to age—spit, hydrate and save appetite for the valley floor. This hill is not a dash; it is a slow tilt upward, in altitude and in expectation.
