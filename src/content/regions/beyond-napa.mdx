# Lede

One of the most appealing aspects of using downtown Napa as a travel base is its proximity to the wine regions that surround the town. Just a few miles east is Coombsville. Head the opposite direction and you'll arrive in the windswept Carneros region, or in the rustic hills of Mt. Veeder.

Coombsville is mostly residential, but vineyards tuck into valleys and rolling hills leading east up to Mt. George. Carneros, which straddles Napa and Sonoma counties, is known for its cool climate and proximity to San Pablo Bay, with plenty of vineyards to drive through. Mt. Veeder is marked by rugged, winding roads; its vineyards are hard to see from the road through the trees clinging to the eastern face of the Mayacamas range.

# Where to Taste

## Featured Wineries

### Etude Winery
- **Address:** 1250 Cuttings Wharf Road
- **Website:** etudewines.com
- **Image:** /images/beyond-napa/wineries/beyond-napa-winery-etude-winery-landscape.jpg
- **ImagePortrait:** /images/beyond-napa/wineries/beyond-napa-winery-etude-winery-portrait.jpg

Etude's tasting room, in a stone building and former distillery, is an inviting, relaxed backdrop for its wines, especially the single-vineyard expressions of Pinot Noir and Cabernet Sauvignon. Enjoy a seated experience or simply wander the grounds.

### Favia Wines
- **Address:** 2031 Coombsville Road
- **Website:** faviawines.com
- **Image:** /images/beyond-napa/wineries/beyond-napa-winery-favia-wines-landscape.jpg
- **ImagePortrait:** /images/beyond-napa/wineries/beyond-napa-winery-favia-wines-portrait.jpg

The ultimate intimate experience: a visit to the homestead of vintners Annie Favia and Andy Erickson. Private tastings take place in a cabin that doubles as the winery's lab and include the historic former Antonio Carbone Wine Cellar and Italian Garden, dating to the 1870s. Historic artifacts are on display, but the spectacular wines overshadow them.

### Hudson Ranch
- **Address:** 5398 Sonoma Highway
- **Website:** hudsonranch.com
- **Image:** /images/beyond-napa/wineries/beyond-napa-winery-hudson-ranch-landscape.jpg
- **ImagePortrait:** /images/beyond-napa/wineries/beyond-napa-winery-hudson-ranch-portrait.jpg

A magical spot, with landscaping of unusual succulents and a flower, fruit and vegetable garden with larger-than-life gourds that offer a touch of Willy Wonka. The meticulous vineyards are surrounded by rugged oaks, with stunning views and wildlife across the 2,000-acre ranch. Ask about picnics and hikes.

# Where to Eat

## Featured Restaurants

### Bistro Don Giovanni
- **Address:** 4110 Howard Lane
- **Website:** bistrodongiovanni.com

An institution since 1993, serving exceptional rustic Italian to vintners and tourists. A large covered patio and garden make it one of the most romantic spots around, especially on a summer night. Handmade pastas, fried anchovy-stuffed olives, wood-fired pizzas, veal parmigiana and Mamma Concetta's meatballs on soft polenta.

### Carabao
- **Address:** 145 C Gasser Drive
- **Website:** carabaonapa.com
- **Image:** /images/beyond-napa/restaurants/beyond-napa-restaurant-carabao-landscape.jpg
- **ImagePortrait:** /images/beyond-napa/restaurants/beyond-napa-restaurant-carabao-portrait.jpg

The hot new eatery in town, serving high-end, inventive Filipino cuisine in an intimate space. Chef Jade Cunningham brings techniques from The French Laundry and Meadowood to dishes like pork lumpia and Kare Kare stew with oxtail croquette, plus deviled egg adobo and crispy pork sisig tacos with a sunny-side quail egg. The compact wine list leans on German Rieslings and aromatic whites.

# Sidebar: A Carneros Adventure

Several places around Napa feel completely distinct from the rest of the valley. Carneros stands out with its own microclimate, aromas, ambience and energy. Driving through Carneros typically means traveling east to west rather than Napa's usual north-to-south. Rolling hills define the landscape; curving roads invite leisurely drives, and on clear days you can glimpse San Pablo Bay. Maritime winds sweep through, and towering eucalyptus windbreaks release a camphorlike fragrance.

**Boon Fly Café** 4048 Sonoma Highway. Start the Napa side of Carneros with donuts, specifically the famous ones at Boon Fly Café. The bright red, barn-style restaurant welcomes guests through a separate entrance off Sonoma Highway. Order the chicken and waffles, Dungeness crab Benedict or corned beef hash, but save room for the donuts.

**Bouchaine Vineyards** 1075 Buchli Station Road. Less than 10 minutes away, Bouchaine offers sweeping bay views on a clear day. The wines are precise and aromatic and the winery hosts dynamic educational programs. On Friday mornings, the Falconry in the Garden experience stands out: a master falconer introduces guests to raptors and explains how they partner with vineyard teams, all while you enjoy a glass of wine.

**Domaine Carneros** 1240 Duhig Road. Just five minutes away, Domaine Carneros rises from a hilltop like a fairy-tale château. Its reputation rests on exceptional sparkling wines. Two experiences stand out: the Art of Sabrage, opening a bottle with a sword, and the Ultimate Caviar Experience, pairing sparkling wines with Tsar Nicoulai Caviar, gourmet potato chips, toast points and crème fraîche.
